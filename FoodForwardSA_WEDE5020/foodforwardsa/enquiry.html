What happens next

Where each path leads

Donating food

A member of the foodbanking team will confirm collection details, food-safety requirements, and the nearest warehouse to your business.

Donating funds

You'll receive banking details and, for regular donors, information on Section 18A tax certificates issued at year end.

Volunteering

Warehouse tasks include picking, packing, sorting and gardening. You'll be matched to a shift at the warehouse nearest your selected province.
