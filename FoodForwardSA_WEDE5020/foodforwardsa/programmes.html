Our work

Four programmes, one supply route

Each programme moves food or support along the same basic path — from surplus, to storage, to the people who need it.

Programme 01

Foodbanking & food rescue

The core of FoodForward SA's model. Quality, in-date surplus food is collected from farmers, manufacturers and retailers, then sorted, stored and dispatched from nine warehouses around the country.

Every beneficiary organisation on the network is vetted before onboarding, and receives an unannounced quarterly monitoring visit to confirm food reaches the people it's meant for.

By the numbers
  • 20,732 tonnes recovered in the 2024/25 financial year
  • 9 warehouses across all nine provinces
  • 2,500+ vetted beneficiary organisations
  • 91.3% of distributed food rated nutritious
Programme 02

FoodShare

A technology-enabled matching platform that connects surplus food directly with beneficiary organisations, cutting the time between "this food is about to go to waste" and "this food is on its way to someone".

Why it matters

Speed is the whole game

Surplus food has a short shelf life. FoodShare exists because a warehouse-only model can't always move fast enough — some donations need to go straight from a retailer's back door to a community kitchen the same day.

Programme 03

Mother & Child Nutrition Programme

Targeted nutritional support for at-risk pregnant women and young children, recognising that early-life nutrition gaps have effects that ordinary foodbanking alone doesn't fully address.

Who it reaches

Delivered through partner clinics and early-childhood development centres already inside the beneficiary organisation network, so it adds to existing relationships rather than building new ones from scratch.

Programme 04

Disaster relief support

When flooding or other disasters disrupt communities, FoodForward SA redirects part of its warehouse capacity to emergency response, working alongside its existing beneficiary network rather than standing up a separate system.

Recent example

Flood response

Communities affected by severe flooding received sustained relief support channelled through existing partner organisations already vetted and active in those areas.

Want to be part of the route?

Whether you're donating food, giving funds or offering time at a warehouse, every contribution enters at a different point in the same chain.
