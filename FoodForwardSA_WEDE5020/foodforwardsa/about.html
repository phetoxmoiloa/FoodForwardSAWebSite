About FoodForward SA

Four grassroots projects. One national foodbank.

FoodForward SA is the largest food redistribution organisation in Africa — but it didn't start that way.

History

How the organisation came together

Pre-2009

Four separate projects

Food Redistribution, the Lions Food Project, the Robin Good Initiative and the Johannesburg Foodbank each worked independently to move surplus food to people who needed it.

2009

FoodBank South Africa founded

The four projects amalgamated into a single national organisation, established specifically to address widespread food and nutrition insecurity.

Later

Renamed FoodForward SA

The organisation adopted its current name and grew a warehouse presence in all nine provinces.

Today

Largest food redistributor in Africa

A certified member of the Global Foodbanking Network, working with over 2,500 vetted beneficiary organisations nationwide.

Mission & vision

Why the work exists

Mission

To recover quality, within-date surplus food from the consumer goods supply chain and distribute it to a network of vetted beneficiary organisations serving vulnerable communities.

Vision

A South Africa where good food is never wasted while people go hungry — where surplus and need are connected efficiently, safely and with dignity.

Who this is for

Everyone the website needs to speak to

The organisation's audience isn't one group — the site has to work for all of them without the pages fighting each other.

Donors

Individuals and businesses giving funds or surplus stock, who need to trust their contribution is tracked and used well.

Volunteers

People wanting hands-on warehouse work — sorting, packing, and helping move food along the route.

Beneficiary organisations

Community kitchens, ECD centres and shelters applying to join the vetted BO network.

Leadership

Led from the warehouse floor up

FoodForward SA is headed by Managing Director Andy du Plessis, who has been the organisation's public voice on South Africa's food-loss and food-insecurity crisis, including recent research published in partnership with UCT's Southern Africa Labour and Development Research Unit.

Recognition

Global Foodbanking Network

FoodForward SA is a certified member of the Global Foodbanking Network, the international body connecting foodbanks across more than 40 countries.
